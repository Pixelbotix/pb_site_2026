Frequently Asked Questions

Which accounting and taxation courses are available?
The brochure lists Basic Accounting & Tally Prime, Tally Advanced, GST Computation, GST Return Filing, Income Tax Computation, ITR Filing, TDS Computation, TDS Return Filing, Payroll, PF & ESI, Business Taxation, Financial Statements & MIS, Excel, Word and PowerPoint.
What is the difference between the two learning options?
The self-paced option is designed for independent online study. The online-with-mentor option provides a mentor-supported format. Confirm the specific support and live-session arrangements for your selected course before enrolment.
Can students outside Chennai enrol?
Online courses are intended to make learning accessible beyond Chennai, including learners across Tamil Nadu, Kerala and other Indian states. Availability depends on the course and delivery format.
Are specialised combinations available?
The brochure lists Junior Accounts Executive, Senior Accounts Executive, Finance & Taxation Executive, HR Specialisation and Tax Specialisation. The brochure specifies a three-month duration for these programmes.
Will I receive a certificate?
The brochure describes a certificate of completion with QR-code verification. Confirm the completion requirements and certificate terms for the selected programme.
How can I enquire about course fees?
Contact the PixelBotix Eduverse team on WhatsApp. Share the course name and preferred learning mode so the team can confirm the current fee, availability and enrolment procedure.
